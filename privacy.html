Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 16, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Romo Cloud ("Romo Cloud," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you interact with our website, our AI phone assistant, and our text messaging program. By using our services, you agree to the practices described here.

Information We Collect

  • Contact information you provide, such as your name and phone number, when you call a business that uses Romo Cloud to book or ask about an appointment.
  • Appointment details you share, such as your preferred date, time, and the reason for your visit.
  • Call and message data, such as call records, message logs, and records of your consent to receive text messages.
  • Website usage data, such as basic device and browser information collected when you visit romocloud.com.

How We Use Information

  • To answer calls, schedule appointments, and confirm or follow up on those appointments on behalf of the business you contacted.
  • To send you text messages related to your appointment, such as confirmations, reminders, and follow-ups.
  • To operate, maintain, and improve our services.
  • To comply with legal obligations and maintain records of consent.

Text Messaging (SMS)

When you call a business that uses Romo Cloud and provide your phone number to book or ask about an appointment, you consent to receive SMS text messages from Romo Cloud related to that appointment, including confirmations, reminders, and follow-ups.

Reply STOP to opt outReply HELP for help

Message frequency varies based on your appointment activity. Message and data rates may apply, depending on your mobile carrier and plan. You can opt out at any time by replying STOP, and you can get assistance by replying HELP or emailing us at the address below.

Your consent to receive text messages is specific to the business you contacted through Romo Cloud and is not a condition of any purchase.

No mobile information will be shared with or sold to third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Mobile opt-in data and consent are never shared with any third parties.

How We Share Information

We share the information you provide with the business you contacted, so that it can schedule and honor your appointment. We may also share information with service providers who help us operate our services (for example, our calendar, telephony, and messaging providers), and only to the extent needed to perform those services.

We do not sell your personal information. As stated above, mobile information and SMS consent are never shared with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

Data Retention

We retain the information described here for as long as needed to provide our services, maintain records of consent for text messaging, and meet our legal and operational requirements. We then delete or anonymize it.

Your Choices and Rights

  • Opt out of text messages at any time by replying STOP.
  • Request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information by contacting us.
  • Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under applicable privacy laws.

Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect the information we hold. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children's Privacy

Our services are intended for adults and are not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the effective date above and post the updated policy on this page.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or our text messaging program, contact us at info@romocloud.com.
